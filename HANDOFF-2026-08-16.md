## The three things worth carrying forward

**1 · A threshold with no gap is not always a tuned threshold.** R81's population — pairs of
observations that overlap heavily — sits in a continuum, and two attempts to separate it by
choosing a cut point (room over box width; overlap in points) found no gap, which is where C24's
repairs died. What worked was giving up on the cut point and finding the *structural* statement:
two boxes on one line that overlap by more than half of the narrower one are not two fragments,
they are two readings of the same ink. The corpus then does a different job — not choosing the
constant, but showing it is not on a knife edge: of 852 pairs, 779 share at most 0.47 of the
narrower box and 70 share at least 0.53, **three** lie in between, and moving the constant from
0.5 to 0.4 or 0.6 changes the verdict on six of them.

**2 · An instrument that cannot move is worse than no instrument.** Every column of `score-corpus`
and `score-line-separation` reads **identical** across R82 on all eight invariant-3 documents,
while the extracted text gains seventeen spaces. Nothing in `Tools/` could count a weld, which is
most of why A1.1 sat open — and `score-corpus`'s `end=` column is *structurally unable* to see
R81, because over a run drawn at 5% of its box the right-hand probe rectangle is answered by the
line above. Two instruments came out of this session: `Tools/score-run-width` (property (c), and
the ceiling that fights it) and `welded=W/N` in `score-line-separation` (property (d), counted
for the first time).

**3 · The obvious fix for C23 was wrong in two different ways, both measured.** Its own entry
proposed carrying a `/CropBox` through `Flattener.flatten` and into `JBIG2.assemble`. On the
rebuild routes recognition reads **whole-sheet bitmaps**, so a crop box on the intermediate makes
`compose` map every observation into the trimmed rectangle and shifts every run on the page. And
`qpdf --overlay` wraps both layers in form XObjects whose `/BBox` is the destination's **crop**
box and centres that box on the media box — measured, the page image came out translated by
(50, 96) with everything outside the crop clipped away for good, which is invariant 1's harm
arriving through the fix for C23.

## What C23 cost, and the decision that is the owner's

A document that hides part of any sheet behind a crop box **no longer takes the JBIG2 route**.
There is nowhere else to put the crop: it cannot be present when qpdf runs, and neither
CGPDFContext nor PDFKit can rewrite those pages afterwards without dropping `/JBIG2Decode`
(measured, 7,391 → 13,401 bytes with the compression gone).

- **16 of 233 corpus documents**, 627 of 16,987 pages, **6.8% of the corpus by bytes**.
- Measured on `Canby_1929`, four sampled pages: **91,118 → 206,002 bytes, 2.26x**.

The alternative is written up in C23's entry rather than left to be rediscovered: qpdf ships
`fix-qdf` precisely so a `--qdf` file can be edited and repaired, which would let the crop be
inserted after the overlay and keep the compression, at the cost of two more qpdf invocations, a
second binary from the same package, and hand-editing page dictionaries. **Refused at 6.8% of
the corpus; revisit if that share moves.**
