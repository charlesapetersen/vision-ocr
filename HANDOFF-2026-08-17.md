## The five things worth carrying forward

**1 · R57's caveat was wrong, and looking at three pages settled it in ten minutes.**
The entry said of its three band-dwelling corpus pages: *"Those three have not been
rendered and compared; that is the next step."* Rendered: `Ehrenreich_2000` p9 is a
Laurie Simmons photograph coming out as a black blob, and the two `HarpersMagazine-1938`
pages are dense type that 1-bit renders perfectly. One of each. **A step described in
the register as "the next step" and never taken was the whole question**, and it cost
ten minutes against the four measurement rounds spent around it. `Schwaller`'s 87
photograph pages — a much larger instance nobody had found — turned up in the same hour.

**2 · The thing that separates a drawing from show-through is not shape. It is place.**
Four luminance rounds were refused on the three-way blindness R56 records, and this
session refused *two more shape rounds* for the same reason: at any usable reduction,
real show-through merges into components taller than a line and emptier than a block, so
size and fill cannot tell it from a drawing. What works is that show-through lies **in**
the page's own type — it is the reverse page's type at the same measure — and a figure
sits where the type is not. `maximumInkUnderADrawing` is that question: holding
everything else fixed it takes `Doermann_1967` from 24 pages to 1 and `Ibson_2006` from
93 to 0, while the drawing fixture does not move by a thousandth — its plate has no type
under it at all. On the shipped constants both documents are at **0**.

**3 · An acceptance test written first did its job four times, and never the way it was
meant to.** `FEATURES.md` step 1 said to write it before the signal. What it caught: a
first draft that passed all six fixtures and moved **228 of `Himanen_2001`'s 255
pages**; a second that passed and moved 30 pages of `Boltanski_2006` — R38's document,
the one that inflated 9.45x when misrouted; a third whose ink-area gate silently dropped
a reversed-out advertisement that 1-bit destroys; and a fourth that fired on scanner-edge
blobs. None of the four is visible from the fixtures.
`Tools/score-routing-census.swift` is the instrument — all 16,987 pages, one row each,
and a diff names the ones that moved. It settled at **125 pages over 21 documents, every
one 1-bit → picture and none the other way**, with 87 of them the `Schwaller`
photographs. Read R57's corpus section: the bar said *no* page moves, and saying so
plainly is better than restating the bar afterwards.

**4 · The scoped read was worth more than the build.** `RESEARCH-shape-signals.md`
corrected the analysis resolution by 4x — 37.5 ppi is a *seed* in Leptonica, never a
decision surface, and `FEATURES.md` had specified the 40 DPI routing thumbnail — and
established the negative result that stopped a seventh round: **no published method
separates a pale drawing from show-through.** DjVuLibre contains no separator at all.
Read §7 and §6.1 before designing anything here.

**5 · Every number in a doc comment gets recomputed by the review, and two of mine were
wrong.** One table came from a superseded 75-cells-per-inch draft; one cell count took
the page inset off once instead of once at each end. Both were in comments arguing that
a constant was safe.

## What the review of this diff found, because it is the sixth round running

Two agents, different lenses, and both found real defects in code written minutes
earlier — another round in which reviewing the previous round's work found real defects
in it, and this register has not yet had one that did not.

- **A trap I introduced.** `Int((dpi / markCellsPerInch).rounded())` on a DPI that
  descends from what the file declares: `MediaBox [0 0 1e-16 1e-16]` with a 2000 px
  image gives 1.44e21, which is 4 megapixels and passes every gate, and the conversion
  is an **uncatchable** crash that takes every concurrent file with it. This is A7.1 at
  a sixth site in one file, found by CONTRIBUTING 4b's own suggested grep.
- **A bound that did not bind.** `markCellCeiling`'s loop tested the *floored* product,
  so a 20,000,000 x 1 render floored the height to zero and allocated 8.75 million cells
  against a ceiling of four — in the constant whose own comment said it had been "caught
  before it shipped".
- **A page border read as a picture.** `largeMarkTone` gated on bounding-box area, so a
  3 px rule round the edge of a sheet qualified and the tone of everything it *encloses*
  was then measured — 1.54x the whole-sheet figure on a synthetic framed page. It takes
  two terms now, size *and* fill, and the paragraph below is why it is not just ink.
- **The census's own diff recipe was wrong.** Rows were streamed in completion order
  while the header claimed input order, so the `diff` the file prescribes reported
  **5,370 changed lines over 210 changed pages**. In the one instrument whose purpose is
  naming the pages that moved.
- **The fixture could not see the constant it was justifying.** `pale-drawing` sits on
  bare paper, so `maximumInkUnderADrawing` reads the same at every value including zero.
  `pale-chart` was added for that — a chart with its own axis numerals inside the plot
  frame, which is the ordinary layout of a figure in a book — and it immediately failed,
  because the statistic summed *ink* and a chart is thin. That is what moved the signal
  from a coverage sum to the extent of the largest mark.
- **Two published tables that did not reproduce**, both arguing that a constant was
  safe: one came from a superseded 75-cells-per-inch draft, one took the page inset off
  once instead of once at each end. The habit that catches these is recomputing every
  arithmetic claim in a doc comment from the code, and it is worth doing to your own. A
  third was found the same way afterwards, by me rather than by an agent — the
  justification for `largeMarkShare` named a page that the constant does not in fact
  decide, and the corpus said so in one query.
- **A constant nothing could see.** `const/minimumMarkContrast` **SURVIVED** a green
  1,118-check suite: its justification is 228 pages of `Himanen_2001` and every fixture's
  marks were either 47 levels below the paper or absent, so no check could tell whether
  it did anything. `faint-marks` — the same drawing at eleven levels — kills it. **Three
  of the ten new mutants have been run; the other seven have not**, and that is written
  in R57 rather than left to be assumed.

**And two things the reviews did not catch, which the corpus did.** Gating the tone
question on a component's own *ink* — the fix for the page-frame finding — silently
dropped `Scott_TK` p13, a reversed-out advertisement that 1-bit really does destroy;
and the size gate that removes four `Doermann_1967` scanner-edge false positives removes
that advertisement with them. Both were settled by rendering the pages and choosing the
direction that keeps content. **A review finding's first fix is not automatically
right**, which is the same lesson as C23's refusal in the last hand-off.
