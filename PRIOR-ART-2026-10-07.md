## 2. Models like Churro: there is no open successor or rival for English print

- **Churro has nothing newer.** `stanford-oval` still has only `churro-3B`; the toolkit (v0.3.0, Apr 2026) added
  backends and leaderboard rows, not weights. CHURRO-DS was re-uploaded 20 Feb 2026 (a metadata refresh).
- **Churro's own leaderboard** [checked: `benchmark_results.json` in github.com/stanford-oval/Churro], CHURRO-DS test,
  normalised Levenshtein similarity, printed / handwritten / English printed:

  | model | printed | handwritten | English printed |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Gemini 3 Flash | 82.6 | 66.5 | 93.4 |
  | Churro 3B | 82.3 | 70.1 | 91.0 |
  | dots.mocr | 81.2 | 55.0 | 87.0 |
  | Qwen3-VL-8B | 76.6 | 48.5 | 87.9 |
  | NuMarkdown-8B | 72.7 | 51.2 | 83.0 |
  | RolmOCR | 67.2 | 49.0 | 88.4 |

  These are the Churro authors' figures on Churro's own benchmark.
- **Historical-specific open models found**, none a better fit than Churro for English 19th–20th c. print:
  `wjbmattingly/nara-qwen-3.5-2b` [checked: exists, Apache-2.0, 22 Sep 2026] (Qwen3.5-2B fine-tuned on 38,756 NARA
  Revolutionary War pension pages: forms, cards, cursive; authors' CER 0.269; a cheap fit-test for typescripts and
  forms, not newspapers); `Carlosian/Qwen3-VL-4B-Trithemius` (Latin blackletter, about 1500); Churro derivatives
  `Ericu950/swedish-print-ocr-3b` (Swedish Fraktur, gated), `fabreupeixoto/churro-3b-octopus`, `textum-org/churro3b`
  (no cards); `NealCaren/qwen3-vl-*-ocr-detailed-merged-16bit` (no cards; training data unknown); narrow LoRAs on
  LightOnOCR (Polish gazetteer, one Italian book) and dots.mocr (Korean, `dhchoi/bandit-ocr`). Line-level only:
  dh-unibe German XIX, Medusa, NealCaren TrOCR/Kraken newspaper line models, Kraken PP-OCRv6, PERO-OCR. Not open:
  Transkribus Text Titan, `MarcoPerson/ssm-ocr-benchmark` (weights not yet released).

## 3. Benchmarks for historical OCR, and their leaders

- **CHURRO-DS** (EMNLP 2025): above.
- **socOCRbench v3** (Dasanaike; noahdasanaike.github.io/posts/sococrbench.html; private 280 pages): best open model
  at 10B or under is dots.ocr 1.5 (= dots.mocr), printed NES 0.755, overall 0.478; Qwen3-VL-8B 0.707 / 0.473;
  Qwen3.5-4B 0.642 / 0.426; PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 0.658 / 0.394; GLM-OCR 0.610 / 0.368. Churro is not on it.
- **NewsBench** (github.com/nealcaren/newsbench; 15 scored LoC newspaper pages 1850s–1919, 1−CER) [checked:
  scoresheet.md]: GLM-OCR on DocLayout-YOLO regions 0.970; PaddleOCR-VL on DocLayout-YOLO regions 0.970; GLM-OCR,
  another run, 0.959; best PaddleX-cut runs 0.92–0.94; Tesseract on DocLayout regions 0.919. Whole-page systems
  (newspaper-ocr README): Gemini Flash Lite 0.820, dots.ocr 0.533, several open models below zero. Churro is not on it.
- **Reading the Archive by Machine** (Clifford et al., July 2026, zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21297825): BLN600 cropped
  19th-c. newspaper excerpts CER Chandra 2 0.58%, GLM-OCR 0.67%, olmOCR 2 1.93%; whole multi-column pages split the
  tools (chunk F1 Infinity-Parser2-Pro 90.2, Chandra 2 83.2, GLM-OCR 44.7); olmOCR modernises spelling.
- **olmOCR-Bench old scans**: Chandra 2 51.1, dots.mocr 48.2, olmOCR 2 47.7, PaddleOCR-VL v1 37.8.
- **FineBooks** (HF blog, Aug 2026; 18th–19th c. books, no newspapers): dots.mocr 97.6%, OvisOCR2 0.9B 96.9%,
  PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 96.1%, olmOCR-2 95.7%.
- **Character Error Vector** (Bourne et al., arXiv 2604.06160; a 19th-c. newspaper): every layout-then-read
  pipeline beat every end-to-end model; best pipeline docling-layout-heron + PaddleOCR.
- **Bourne 2025** (arXiv 2502.14901): DocLayout-YOLO regions read by Pixtral-12B, median CER 0.01 on 19th-c. English
  periodicals; mean 0.27 from repetition loops.

## 4. The finding that matters most: cut newspapers into regions first

Four independent sources (NewsBench, Character Error Vector, Bourne 2025, and the Harvard IDI / Boston Public Library
pipeline, arXiv 2608.18972, which chose crop-level OCR for 1.47M newspaper scans) agree that on dense newspaper pages
the layout step decides the result and whole-page readers degrade. This bake-off read newspapers as the truth set's
crops, not with a layout model; nobody has measured Churro on region crops from a layout model or on NewsBench.

Layout models that run on this Mac:
- `juliozhao/DocLayout-YOLO-DocStructBench` [checked: exists, Apache-2.0 tag; the code repo is AGPL-3.0] — the
  detector in NewsBench's top rows; a Core ML conversion exists.
- PP-DocLayoutV3 (Apache-2.0, polygons plus reading order in one pass) — `pp_doclayout_v3` is in this lab's
  mlx-vlm 0.7.4 [checked: module list]; no newspaper figure yet.
- `institutional/institutional-newspapers-segmenter-yolo26x` — trained on 1795–1930 US newspapers (mAP50-95 0.901,
  authors'); gated, non-commercial, no redistribution: testable, not shippable. Its reading-order rule (HDBSCAN
  column clustering) is simple to reimplement.
- Avoid the American Stories detector: it under-segments dense pages (NewsBench MEMO.md).

   readers on it, whole-page and on DocLayout-YOLO regions.

   Boston Public Library's choice for newspaper crops.
3. **PaddleOCR-VL-1.6** (`PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR-VL-1.6` [checked: Apache-2.0], 0.96B; official GGUF; `paddleocr_vl`
   in mlx-vlm) — a region reader only (0.970 on NewsBench regions, weak whole-page).
4. **DocLayout-YOLO and PP-DocLayoutV3** as the region cutter for `ocr-hybrid-proto`.
5. Cheap extras: `wjbmattingly/nara-qwen-3.5-2b` (typescripts and forms); NuMarkdown-8B-Thinking (`numind`, MIT,
