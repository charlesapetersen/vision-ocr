# Whole-codebase review, 2026-08-14

A sequential adversarial review of the codebase, one area at a time, at the owner's
request. **This file is the durable record of what was found, not a summary of what was
fixed** — each finding says whether it was verified by running code or only reasoned
about, and nothing here has been fixed unless the entry says so.

Findings that get fixed graduate into `BUGS.md` with an R-number and are struck through
here. Findings that are refused stay here with the reason.

Read `CONTRIBUTING.md` §3 before acting on any of these: **the instrument is wrong more
often than the code**, and this project has a long history of "confirmed" findings that
were measurement artefacts. Every entry below that is marked VERIFIED was verified by the
reviewer running code; that is not the same as being independently reproduced, and where a
finding proposes a constant change, the four invariant-3 probes have **not** been re-run.

## Area 1 — `Sources/SearchableWriter.swift` (the text layer)

ARCHITECTURE.md names this risk #1: four properties must hold at once and each has
historically been broken by a fix to another. Two real defects, both measured on real
corpus pages.

### A1.1 · Words weld again — property (d) fails in a measured band. VERIFIED, OPEN

**31 welded word pairs across 21 real newspaper pages**, read back out of the composed
file with PDFKit. `ity`+`companies`, `female`+`member`, `Greece`+`(AP)_The`. This is C18's
symptom — `valuablestudy` — returned for a class of cases.

**Mechanism.** `reserveEms` (the constant that holds property (d) open) can only act
through `rightLimit`, and `rightLimit` only considers a neighbour that `isSameVisualLine`
accepts: baselines within `sameLineBaselineFraction` (0.4) × **min**(a.height, b.height).
The `min` is deliberate — C20 chose it so a tall display numeral cannot claim a body line
two rows away — but it means a pair where one fragment is short gets a tolerance of only
1.4–2.1 pt, while **PDFKit's own line grouping is far looser (~8 pt)**. In the band
between the two, no reserve is opened, the run is drawn to its full box width, and the
words weld. `headroom` does not cover it either: side-by-side fragments have no horizontal
overlap, so its test skips them.

Synthetic boundary, verified: two 9 pt fragments, gap ≤ 2 pt, baseline offset **3.7 pt**
against a tolerance of 3.6 → `the valuablestudy of it`. At 3.5 pt → correct. Setting
`sameLineBaselineFraction = 1.0` fixes every welded case in the sweep, which identifies
the gate as the cause.

**Do not simply raise the constant.** It is shared with `headroom` on purpose (C20 unified
them), and loosening it globally makes `headroom` skip real vertical neighbours — which is
the overlap defect C20 existed to fix. The reserve direction needs its own, looser
predicate, or `max` height for that direction only. **All four invariant-3 probes must be
re-run**, and nothing in the suite currently covers this band: `mutate.py` kills
`const/sameLineBaselineFraction` only with a "the calibrated value is 0.4" pin.

`Sources/SearchableWriter.swift:1006`, with `:929` and `:947-952`.

### A1.2 · A whole line can be drawn at 5% of its width, silently. VERIFIED, OPEN

`rightLimit` only accepts a neighbour whose left edge is right of this run's, so
`allowed > 0` **unconditionally** — which means the guard the comment at `:1013-1014`
claims ("`allowed > 0` already refuses a heavy overlap") cannot fire. When Vision emits a
same-line fragment starting a few points right of a wide fragment's left edge, `allowed`
is a couple of points, the font size hits its 0.5 pt floor, and the line is drawn as a
~1.5 pt sliver. No `Unplaced` is reported.

Verified on `testdocs/newspaperArticle/___ 3.pdf` p6: a 264 pt-wide, 60-character line
drawn **13.36 pt wide (5% of the ink)** and 1.5 pt tall, because its nearest same-line
neighbour starts 3.62 pt to its right. Probing the output: the leftmost 6 pt selects the
whole line, the right half selects *other lines'* text, the bottom 3 pt selects nothing —
the line is effectively unhighlightable. Rate over 21 newspaper pages: **28 of 6,997
fragments below 15% of their box width, 62 below 50%**, several at 0.0%.

Characters are still in the content stream, so *search* is unaffected; property (c) is
silently lost. The fix is to make the comment's intent real — ignore a "neighbour" that
leaves this run no usable width — rather than clamping toward zero.

`Sources/SearchableWriter.swift:1102`.

### A1.3 · Smaller, real, low value

- `observations(fromJSONLines:)` `:161` and `observations(fromJSONAt:)` `:181` have **no
  callers** since recognition came in-process, and are divergent siblings: one refuses an
  undecodable line and an empty result, the other refuses neither. Dead code that would
  mislead the next reuse.
- `:997-998` — `myHeight` and `myBaseline` computed and never used in `rightLimit`
  (compiler warns); leftovers from C20.
- `:402` — `readOutline` indexes `doc.index(for: page)` with no `< pageCount` bound while
  its mirror `copyOutline:558` has one. Harmless today because `JBIG2.flatten` drops an
  out-of-range index. **This is the R23/R29 mirror shape** and worth closing on sight.
- `copyOutline` allows an effective depth of 31 against `readOutline`'s 32.

### A1.4 · Unexplained, flagged rather than guessed at

7 of the 31 real welds involve a pair that *is* same-visual-line, where the reserve did
apply and the drawn gap was 2.6–7.3 pt (0.8 em in the dissected case). A two-fragment page
with the same geometry produced a correct space, so the mechanism involves PDFKit's line
grouping over the whole page. Not pinned.

### Checked and found sound (area 1)

Verified by running: the two page boxes on the **non-rebuild** path with crop ≠ media,
across all four rotations, a non-zero media origin, and a crop half outside the media box —
`cropRegion` always matches the box the recogniser normalised to. Per-page boxes across a
multi-page document with a rotated page in the middle: no box leaks between pages
(invariant 4). C10's claim that publishing at the media box keeps marks outside the crop.
**`deduplicated` is inert** — 233 corpus documents × 3 pages removed zero observations,
matching ARCHITECTURE's claim. Non-Latin scripts through the Helvetica-only run: Cyrillic,
Greek, CJK, Arabic, Hebrew, Thai, Devanagari and combining marks all place and round-trip.
Unplaced completeness: every early return in `draw` is reported, and NaN/±inf coordinates
are refused rather than drawn.

Reasoned, not run: outline depth bounds and cycle termination; `mapSingleAxis`'s axis
choice for each rotation; `headroomFactor`'s inverse-ness matching its prose.

## Area 2 — `Sources/Model.swift` (pipeline, publish, cancellation) and `Sources/JBIG2.swift`

`JBIG2.assemble` came out of this **sound** — see the checked list. Everything below is in
`Model.swift`, and two of the three are about publishing the wrong thing.

### ~~A2.1 · `publish` cannot replace an existing file on another volume~~ — FIXED, `BUGS.md` R59

`publish` uses `replaceItemAt(output, withItemAt: staged)` when the destination exists, and
`staged` is always in `NSTemporaryDirectory()` — the boot volume. `replaceItemAt` requires
both items on **one** volume and returns POSIX 18 `EXDEV` otherwise. The `else` branch
(`moveItem`) is fine, because Foundation copies across devices. So **the first run to an
external drive or network share succeeds and every later run over the same output fails**:

```
pass 1: succeeded   901054 bytes, 3 pages
pass 2: failed      "The file “Stiglitz.ocr.pdf” couldn’t be saved in the folder “pubtest”."
```

Verified against the real `makeSearchablePDF` with the output on a mounted volume, on both
HFS+ and APFS. Trigger: output folder on any non-boot volume — or *Save beside each
original* with the originals there — plus an output already present. Correct a setting,
press Start again, and every file in the batch fails with a message that names neither the
cause nor a remedy. Invariant 2 holds (the old file survives; nothing partial is written),
so this is total failure rather than content loss. The same applies to *Use Existing Text*;
plain Extract Text escapes it because it writes atomically to the target.

`Sources/Model.swift:1302-1309`.

### A2.2 · Cancel is observed and the file is published anyway. VERIFIED — searchable path FIXED (R59), text path STILL OPEN

The last cancellation check is at `:1735`; `publish` is at `:1821`. Everything between is
uninterruptible: two `PDFPageCount` reads, the unplaced check, `copyOutline` (a full PDFKit
re-serialisation) and — when *Keep highlights and notes* is on — the transplant's three
qpdf passes. Cancelling in that window publishes the file and reports **`.succeeded`**:

```
cancel fired at: staged.pdf complete (19 pages)
isCancelled: true    outcome: succeeded    published: true, 19 pages
```

Measured window: 0.38 s at 19 pages, 0.73 s at 57 — about 13 ms/page for `copyOutline`
alone, so ≈7 s on a 568-page book, and longer with the transplant. ARCHITECTURE's "three
gates, then publish" and "failure is reported, never published" are both false here. The
existing cancel test only cancels *before* the run starts, so this window is untested.

**The sibling is worse.** `Recogniser.extract` checks `isCancelled` only at the top of each
page, so a cancel during the last page finishes it and writes **straight to the user's
destination with no staging** — and `:1244` then reports `.cancelled`. The log says
"⊘ cancelled" while the previous run's `.txt` has already been replaced. For the
single-page inputs that mode usually gets, that is the whole run.

`Sources/Model.swift:1735 → 1821`, and `:1241-1245`.

### ~~A2.3 · `publish` onto a directory deletes it and its contents~~ — FIXED, `BUGS.md` R59

`fileExists(atPath:)` is true for a directory, and `replaceItemAt` then removes it
recursively. A directory named `scan.ocr.pdf` holding files was destroyed and `publish`
returned success. Low likelihood — `uniqueOutputs` compares its output paths against other
batch paths, never against what is on disk — but it is unannounced destruction of something
that was never OCR output, and one `isDirectory` guard closes it.

`Sources/Model.swift:1305`.

### A2.4 · Smaller, in `JBIG2.swift`

- `:215` — `write` falls back to `?? Data()`, silently emitting **nothing** for a string
  that is not Latin-1-encodable. Unreachable today (every caller-controlled string goes
  through `pdfString`, `trim` or `coordinate`), but `written` would not advance either, so
  the xref would stay self-consistent while an object body was missing — a structurally
  broken PDF with a valid-looking cross-reference table, in the one file whose whole job is
  not producing that. A throw costs nothing.
- `:403` — `%010ld` keeps each xref entry at exactly 20 bytes only below 10 GB; past
  9,999,999,999 the field widens and every entry becomes 21. The `%010d` trap's next
  boundary. Reachable only by ~1,300 pages of 100 MP colour plates.
- `Model.swift:1608, 1630-1631` — the MRC loop reads whole files with `Data(contentsOf:)`
  to compare sizes while `sizeNote` answers the same question from file attributes. Two
  definitions of one idea (C20's shape), the expensive one on the per-page path.

### Checked and found sound (area 2)

**`JBIG2.assemble`, thoroughly.** Xref verified byte-for-byte on a 3-page file mixing
jbig2/jpeg/MRC with a nested outline: all 19 entries 20 bytes, every offset landing on its
`N 0 obj`, `/Size` correct, `qpdf --check` clean. Object numbering survives a variable
`imageCount` MRC page. **Every failure path was made to fail** (§4c) and each cleans up its
partial file: 0 pages; `0×0`, NaN, ±inf, negative and `200001` boxes on page 1 *and* page 2;
a missing stream; an empty stream; a missing MRC layer; an unwritable destination.
`/Length` cannot disagree with the bytes — it is `bytes.count` of the same `Data` emitted.
Outline titles: unbalanced parens and backslashes escaped, non-ASCII as UTF-16BE with BOM,
70,000 characters, 5,000 siblings, out-of-range `pageIndex` dropped rather than left
dangling, non-finite coordinates → `null`. `%.4f` is locale-independent.

**The MRC re-layer loop loses nothing.** Each iteration is independent and self-cleaning;
the JPEG is deleted only after `encoded[index]` is replaced; an encode failure or
`after >= before` leaves the working JPEG in place; a cancel mid-loop breaks into the gate
and reports cancelled. The stencil's declared size cannot drift from the bitmap — both run
the identical `fullBox × rebuildDPI` formula on the same page.

**Cancellation pairing.** All five child-launching sites are inside `RunControl.adopting`;
`adoptedCount == 0` after three real JBIG2 runs including the MRC stencil path. The
annotation transplant opens a fresh scope per qpdf pass, so no scope sees two children.

**Concurrency.** No mutable static reachable from `makeSearchablePDF`: `SearchableWriter`'s
`static var` constants are written only by `Tools/score-*.swift` and the test file, and
`Flattener`'s are `let`. `Runner.locateTool` is race-free and correctly memoises a
*negative* result.
