### C57 · Old print and newspapers are read with more words wrong or missing than the approved on-device models leave — OPEN

*(opened 2026-10-13 for `ocr-integrate-engine`, after the owner approved `OCR-COMPARE-DECISION-2026-10-13.md`'s
recommendations. C41 is the newspaper half of the same defect, measured on whole pages.)*

On the 67-page sample (`OCR-COMPARE-2026-10-13.tsv`, measured), Vision alone leaves 1.20% of the words wrong or
missing on print and 29.6% on newspapers. Falcon-OCR bf16 on align leaves 0.63% on print; PaddleOCR-VL 1.6 4-bit
and Falcon-OCR on regions leave 21.8% and 21.3% on newspapers. The app can run these only through the lab's
shell readers in `ops/ocrlab/`, which no user has. Approved to build: print offers Vision, PaddleOCR-VL regions and
Falcon align; newspapers offer Vision, Vision on regions, PaddleOCR-VL regions and Falcon regions; no fill.

#### Sub-box 1, the runtime plan (2026-10-13)

`OCR-RUNTIME-PLAN-2026-10-13.md`. A standalone CPython 3.12.13 with MLX 0.32.3 and mlx-vlm 0.7.4 and only their
read-time imports, built by `ops/ocrlab/make-runtime.sh`: 439-445 MB on disk, 134 MB as a `.tgz` [measured], against
the lab venv's 829 MB; the models are 1.08 GB (Falcon-OCR) and 0.70 GB (PaddleOCR-VL). Falcon-OCR's whole-page reading and PaddleOCR-VL's 181-region reading of sample `s46`, and
Falcon's of `s01`, are byte-identical to the lab venv's, from a copy moved to a path with a space in it and from a
fresh build [measured]. Every Mach-O in it is linker-signed ad hoc and the app has no hardened runtime, so the
helper can start it as a child process with nothing re-signed; that a self-downloaded file is not quarantined is
reasoned, to be tested in a VM with the downloads. DocLayout-YOLO goes to Core ML, run by the helper, so Vision on
regions needs no runtime. Rejected: an MLX Swift port (would not reproduce the figures; whether MLX Swift has the two
architectures was not checked), the runtime inside the app (+134 MB for everyone), the lab venv as it is.
Next: sub-box 2, the runtime in the helper.

#### Sub-box 2, the runtime in the helper (2026-10-13)

`modelReader` may now name a shipped reader, `falcon-ocr` or `paddleocr-vl`, in place of a path.
`ModelRuntime.command` runs it as `<home>/Runtime/bin/python3 -I <Resources>/read-model.py <repo> …` with the
comparison's limits (whole page: `--seconds 540 --max-side 3300`; crops: `--one-line-each --max-tokens 3000
--seconds 540`), `HF_HOME=<home>/Models` and offline; home is `~/Library/Application Support/Vision OCR`, or
`VISIONOCR_MODEL_HOME`. `Resources/read-model.py` is `read-mlx.py`'s Falcon and PaddleOCR-VL path, exiting 1 when
540 s stops a reading. No runtime, no download, or a shipped reader outside the approved arrangements this build serves
(Falcon-OCR: `align`; `regions` for both waits for the layout model; no `fill`) fails the file, saying which. Measured through `score-gate` built from this tree, Falcon-OCR on align: `s03`'s
published text layer is identical (pdftotext hash) whether read by the lab venv's `read-mlx.py`, by `falcon-ocr`
in-process, or by `falcon-ocr` through the helper (a two-file batch, `s03` and `s05`), and identical to
`ocr-compare-run`'s own `falcon-align` output for both pages; Vision's alone differs on `s03`. PaddleOCR-VL's crop
form is wired and its arguments checked, but it has no caller until ie-layout cuts regions.

#### Sub-box 3, the layout model in the app (2026-10-13)

`LayoutModel`: DocLayout-YOLO converted from its published PyTorch weights to Core ML (`ops/ocrlab/convert-layout.py`,
72 MB at 32 bits, ranged input; run on the CPU, 0.4-0.7 s a page, 393 MB peak in a one-process tool) [measured].
Its boxes and scores match the lab's ONNX build's top 300 to 0.00015 px and 1e-5 on 8 pages. The app makes the
letterbox (cv2's INTER_AREA reproduced pixel for pixel), the anchors and top-300 selection, the stand-in order,
the dedupe and the size filter, and writes `reader-regions.sh`'s blocks itself (`layoutBlocks`). `modelReader`
`vision`, `paddleocr-vl` or `falcon-ocr` under `regions` now runs with no lab script. [measured] Crops identical to
the lab's on 8 PNG pages (595 crops). Through `score-gate` and the helper, published layers identical to
`ocr-compare-run`'s on 6 of 7: vision-regions s03 s43, paddle-regions s03 s46, falcon-regions s43 s45. vision-regions
s46 differs by 10 of 5,281 words: from the grey JPEG, ImageIO and PIL decode 0.9% of pixels 1 apart, and 4 of 200
crops move 1 px; deterministic run to run. Not chased: the decoder is the platform's, and the figure changes nothing
at that size. The model's weights are AGPL-3.0, so it is a download beside the runtime, not in the app; whether
the release may offer an AGPL download is the owner's call at release. Landed by the next session (the first
died before its suite compiled). Review there found a page under 1,024 px on its longer side (a pocket book at 150 dpi)
put in at its own size with the boxes scaled as if enlarged, silently misplacing every crop: it is enlarged now
as cv2's INTER_AREA enlarges (`areaEnlarge`, within 1 of cv2 5.0 on a 5x4 to 13x9 case, 6 of 117 pixels 1 apart)
[measured]. A model output of the wrong shape fails the file instead of reading as no regions. Next: ie-guard.

#### Sub-box 4, the guard and the fallback (2026-10-13)

`ModelGuard`, inside `modelReading`: `run-guarded.sh`'s three limits, sampled every 0.5 s. The reader's process
group's physical footprint (`proc_pid_rusage`, the number `footprint` prints, Metal buffers included) may not
pass `modelMemoryGB`, a new setting carried to the helper (`--model-memory-gb`): 5 GB on 16 GB or more, 5/16 of
the Mac below (2.5 GB on 8 GB), the slider `ocr-integrate-settings` will give a control. Critical memory pressure
or swap grown by 2 GB also stops it, and critical pressure keeps it from starting. A shipped reader (`falcon-ocr`,
`paddleocr-vl`, `vision` on regions) stopped by the guard or failing at run time (exit, signal, no text, wrong
count, 600 s) now leaves the page Vision's reading, and the file's log gets one line naming the pages and up to
three reasons; after a stop (guard or time), the rest of the document is not tried, saying so per page. What a
reader needs and lacks (runtime, download, arrangement) is `modelSetup` and still fails the file, and a reader given by path, the lab's, still
fails it on anything, so a measurement never counts Vision's words as a model's. The run report's "Model reader"
row names the limit. [measured] Tests: a perl stand-in holding ~290 MB under a 0.1 GB cap is stopped in seconds
in the app and through the helper, the page publishes Vision's words with the message, and on two pages the
reader starts once. Disabling the verdict (mutant) turns them red: see the commit. [measured from
`guard.tsv`] Of `ocr-compare-run`'s 241 guarded readings none passed 5,120 MB: Falcon regions peaked at 4,892 (`s09`,
228 MB under), Falcon align 3,947, PaddleOCR-VL regions 2,451; so the default stops none of the comparison's pages,
narrowly. Not done: an 8 GB Mac's 2.5 GB default is under Falcon's 3.4-3.9 GB whole page, so there it is stopped on
a document's first page and the rest are not tried; `ocr-integrate-settings` must offer only readers that fit the slider.
Rejected: refusing to start below the cap's worth of reclaimable memory (the lab's `--need-gb`): on a Mac with
sessions open it would refuse most pages that would have fitted. Pressure and swap are machine-wide, so another
program's load can stop a reader; it says so, and nothing is lost. Next: ie-readers.

#### Sub-box 5, the readers by page kind (2026-10-13)

A second pair, `newspaperModelArrangement` / `newspaperModelReader`, read for a page `Recogniser.pageKind` calls a
newspaper (a paste-up is a page `flatten` wrote regions for); unset, newspapers take the main pair, so the lab's
one-pair runs mean what they did. It crosses to the helper. Shipped readers are approved per kind
(`ModelRuntime.offered`): print Falcon-OCR align and PaddleOCR-VL regions; newspapers Vision, PaddleOCR-VL and
Falcon-OCR on regions. A reader approved for neither kind in that arrangement fails the file; one approved only for
the other kind leaves the page Vision's reading, saying so (review: as first written, a print user's Falcon align
failed every file holding one newspaper page). After a stop, a later page is "not tried" only if its kind has a reader.
[measured] `score-gate` from this tree, helper on, all 67 sample pages per run, 67 published each time:
Falcon align on print, 56 of 56 published layers identical to `ocr-compare-run`'s (pdftotext hash); Falcon regions on
the 7 pages called newspapers, 6 identical, s30 3 words worse of 3,251; PaddleOCR-VL regions, 64 of 67 identical, s30
and s62 scoring the same, s45 1 word worse; Vision regions, 4 of 7 identical, s22 the same, s30 1 and s46 3 worse.
These are the grey JPEG pages, the decoder difference sub-box 3 recorded on s46. The four clippings (s17 s40 s41 s44)
are called print, as `PAGE-KIND-2026-10-12.tsv` measured, and take the print reader: under Falcon align they leave
143 words wrong or missing of 4,330, against Vision's 196 and Falcon regions' 136. Newspapers under the routing:
Falcon 21.39% (table 21.35%), PaddleOCR-VL 21.83% (21.83%), Vision regions 26.49% (26.54%). Rejected: keeping the
other kind's reader running after a stop; both run under one memory cap, and pressure or swap is the machine's. Next: `ocr-integrate-settings` gives the
pairs their controls; until then no user can reach them, so the entry stays open.

An "Accurate mode" box in the panel, off by default (`Prefs.accurateMode`). On: a memory slider, 4 to 12 GB in
whole steps (default 5 GB from 16 GB up and now 4 GB below, the lowest step, with a warning; it was 2.5 GB on 8 GB);
one reader per kind, `accurateNewspaperReader` and `accurateReader`, offering only `Prefs.AccurateReader`s approved
for that kind whose memory fits; beside each, the table of section 6 of `OCR-COMPARE-DECISION-2026-10-13.md`, read
from `OCR-COMPARE-2026-10-13.tsv` (wm_share, secs, peak_mb). Downloads are decimal MB from the runtime plan
(Falcon 1,080, PaddleOCR-VL 700, plus the layout model's 72 where the arrangement cuts regions), so they read

the choices into the engine's two pairs, newspapers always set apart (Vision is `.off`, not unset, so a newspaper
never takes Falcon's print `align`); off, the lab's keys are read as before, and no user has them set. A stored choice

#### Settings sub-box 2, the timing run (`ocr-integrate-settings`, is-timing, 2026-10-14)

"Measure on this Mac" under the tables (`TimingRun`, `TimingRunner`). It times Vision on each kind, and each reader that
is downloaded and fits under the slider, through `makeSearchablePDF` under the shipped defaults, on pages it draws
itself: a 12×17 in six-column broadsheet at 200 dpi and two book pages at 300 dpi, grey scans with fixed words. They
are drawn because the sample is third-party. A run is stored only if every leg succeeds with no fallback to Vision.
The table then shows our figure × (this Mac's seconds / `referenceSeconds`, the same run on this Mac). The ratio is
used because the drawn pages are not the sample's mix (Vision takes 40 s on the broadsheet against the sample's
23 s). [measured] Two reference runs here (a third, on a larger seven-column page, was dropped) agreed within 2-5%
per reader: newspapers Vision 39.6 s, Vision regions 51.5, PaddleOCR-VL 77.7, Falcon-OCR 96.2; book pages Vision 2.9,
PaddleOCR-VL 10.1, Falcon-OCR 10.3. A full run takes about 5 minutes. A timing is tied to the reference version and
the Mac's model identifier. Reset to Defaults keeps it. A reader it did not time is marked "(ours)". It refuses to
start beside a batch; a batch started during it is not prevented. Rendered offscreen with a timing 1.5× slower:
every timed speed is 1.5× ours. Rejected: showing the drawn pages' raw seconds (not comparable with the table), and
bundling public-domain scans (curl to the Library of Congress was refused here; the drawn pages are free of licences
and of bundle size). Not measured: how cold the first run after a download is; each leg pays its model's load, as
the reference did. Review: classifier routing comment corrected (a misread page ends the run, saying so).

#### Settings sub-box 3, the downloads (`ocr-integrate-settings`, is-downloads, 2026-10-14)

`ModelDownloads` and `DownloadRunner`. Choosing a reader starts downloading what it lacks: the runtime and the layout
model as one `.tgz` each, and each model file by file from Hugging Face at the revision measured, laid out as the
Hub's cache. The size is stated in the table and on the button, with a progress row and Stop download. Every file is
checked against a pinned size and SHA-256, staged beside its destination, and moved into place whole. Staging left by
a quit is swept at the next download. Nothing starts while the mode is off, and turning it off stops a download.
The table's sizes are now the bytes fetched: runtime 134 MB, layout 65 MB (was 72, its size on disk), PaddleOCR-VL
715 MB (was 700), Falcon-OCR 1,085 MB (was 1,080). [measured] Into an empty scratch home, the two models came live
from Hugging Face (1,085 MB in 98 s, 715 MB in 67 s). The runtime and the layout model came from a local copy of the
archives standing in for the release. Then a timing run over that home ran all seven legs with no fallback, within
2-7% of `referenceSeconds` (e.g. Falcon-OCR newspaper 96.3 s against 96.2). Review: a Stop landing before the URL
task started hung the download for good (reproduced 20/20 with a 10 ms delay; 0/20 with the fix); a restart straight
after a Stop was dropped; a download for a reader no longer chosen had no Stop; a model cached at another revision
counted as installed. All four were fixed with checks. Not covered by a check: a cancel mid-transfer, which `file://`
cannot hold open. Not checked in a VM GUI.
